Game Rules

  • Numbers run correctly from 1 at the bottom to 100 at the top in serpentine order.
  • Players start on 1 and need an exact roll to reach 100.
  • Land on a ladder base to climb; land on a snake head to slide to its tail.
  • Rolling 6 gives another turn.
  • During an active game, the player whose turn it is has 2 minutes to roll. If they do not roll, they are automatically removed and the turn passes to the next remaining player.
  • In a room, the host is the authoritative game state and every connected friend sees the same board, dice result and turns.
Multiplayer uses a peer-to-peer connection through PeerJS signaling. Keep the room creator's browser open while playing.

Game Rules

  • The board uses the correct serpentine sequence: 1–10 on the bottom row, then 11–20 in reverse direction, continuing to 91–100 at the top.
  • Players start on square 1 and must reach square 100.
  • Land on the bottom of a ladder to climb upward.
  • Land on a snake's head to slide down to its tail.
  • An exact roll is required to reach 100.
  • Rolling a 6 grants another turn.
  • The first player to reach 100 wins.
